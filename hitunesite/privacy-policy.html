Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to hitune.in, music.hitune.in, distribution.hitune.in and related Hitune services.

Hitune ("we", "our", "us") is a music streaming and distribution platform operated as a product of Believoo. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe when you use Hitune Music, Hitune Distribution, the Hitune web player, mobile apps, and related subdomains (together, the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Artist/label details you provide for distribution (stage name, label name, payout details, tax/KYC information where required).
  • Profile preferences such as language, avatar and playlists.

Content you submit

  • Audio files, cover artwork, metadata (titles, ISRC/UPC, genre, release date) and related release information submitted through Hitune Distribution.

Usage & device data

  • Listening activity, search queries, playlists and playback history.
  • Device type, operating system, app version, IP address and approximate location.
  • Log data such as access times, pages viewed and crash diagnostics.

Payment data

  • Payments are processed by third-party gateways (e.g., Razorpay, PayU). We receive transaction status and reference IDs — we do not store your full card or banking credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate and improve the Services (streaming, playlists, recommendations).
  • To deliver your music to streaming platforms and digital stores (DSPs) on your behalf.
  • To calculate, report and pay royalties and to produce royalty statements.
  • To process payments, subscriptions and refunds.
  • To communicate with you — service updates, release status, security alerts and, with consent, marketing.
  • To detect fraud, enforce our Terms, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

  • DSPs & stores: release metadata, artist name, artwork and audio are shared with platforms (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, etc.) strictly to distribute your content.
  • Payment processors: limited data required to complete transactions.
  • Group entities: within the Believoo group for infrastructure, support and security purposes.
  • Legal: where required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety and property.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, session management, preferences and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may stop working without them.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and release data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services. Royalty, payout and transaction records are retained as required by tax and accounting laws. You may request deletion of your account; some data may be retained where legally required.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted transport (TLS), hashed passwords, access controls and monitoring — to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work continuously to protect your information.

7. Your Rights

  • Access, correct or update your account information at any time.
  • Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Opt out of marketing communications via unsubscribe links.

8. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect data from children; if discovered, such data is deleted.

9. International Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located in India or other countries where we or our providers operate. We take steps to ensure adequate protection wherever data is processed.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on this page or via email. Continued use of the Services after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Write to us at hello@hitune.in or reach the Believoo team via believoo.com.

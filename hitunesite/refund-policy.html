Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains when refunds apply for Hitune Distribution plans, Hitune Music subscriptions, and other paid services operated by Believoo.

1. Hitune Distribution Plans

  • Before delivery: If you purchase a distribution plan and no release has been submitted or delivered to stores, you may request a full refund within 7 days of purchase.
  • After delivery: Once a release has been delivered to one or more DSPs (Spotify, Apple Music, etc.), the plan fee is considered consumed and is non-refundable.
  • Rejected releases: If a release is rejected during our quality review for reasons within your control (rights issues, bad audio, guideline violations), the plan fee is not refundable — but you may fix and resubmit.

2. Hitune Music Subscriptions

  • Premium subscriptions can be cancelled anytime; access continues until the end of the current billing period.
  • Refunds for the current period are issued only where required by law or in cases of verified billing errors or duplicate charges.
  • In-app purchases made via Google Play / App Store are subject to the respective store's refund policies.

3. Duplicate or Failed Payments

  • Duplicate charges and payments debited but not credited to your account are refunded in full after verification.
  • Refunds are processed back to the original payment method via our payment processor (Razorpay/PayU) and typically reflect within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

4. Royalties & Payouts

  • Royalties earned from DSPs are not "purchases" and are not refundable; they are paid out per your dashboard's payout schedule.
  • If DSPs claw back royalties due to streaming fraud or rights disputes, corresponding amounts may be deducted from your balance.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email hello@hitune.in with subject "Refund Request", including your account email, order/transaction ID, and reason. We review requests within 3–5 business days.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback — most issues resolve faster directly. Unjustified chargebacks may result in account suspension and removal of distributed content.

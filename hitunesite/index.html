How Hitune Distribution Works

From your studio to every streaming platform on the planet — in five simple steps.

01

Create Account

Sign up on Hitune Distribution and pick a plan — Rising Artist, Breakout or Professional.

02

Upload Release

Submit your audio, cover art and metadata — artist name, ISRC, genre, release date.

03

Quality Review

Our team checks audio specs, artwork and rights so stores accept your release first time.

04

Go Live Worldwide

Your music is delivered to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, JioSaavn and 100+ DSPs.

05

Earn Royalties

Track streams and earnings from your dashboard — you keep 100% of your royalties.

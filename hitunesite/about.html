About Hitune

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · Hitune — a Believoo product.

Hitune is the music ecosystem by Believoo — streaming for listeners, worldwide distribution for artists, and creator tools under one brand.

What we build

  • Hitune Music — stream millions of songs, playlists, radio and lyrics on web and Android. music.hitune.in
  • Hitune Distribution — release your music on 100+ global platforms and keep 100% of your royalties. distribution.hitune.in
  • Hitune Web — full-featured web player, no install needed. web.hitune.in
  • Hitune VIBE — our music blog and editorial space. rv.hitune.in

Why we exist

Independent artists deserve the same reach as label artists. Hitune Distribution was built to take a release from your studio to every streaming platform on the planet — without giving up ownership or royalties. Hitune Music gives listeners a fast, modern streaming home for that music.

The Believoo group

Hitune is part of Believoo — a software and hosting company building consumer apps, creator platforms and infrastructure, including believoo.com (cloud & hosting), B-Connect (workspace suite) and GHC (hosting control).

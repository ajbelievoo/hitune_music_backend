Copyright & Content Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Hitune respects the intellectual property rights of artists, labels, songwriters and all creators. This policy explains how ownership works on Hitune and how to report infringement.

1. Content Ownership

  • Artists and rights holders retain 100% ownership of music distributed through Hitune Distribution.
  • By distributing with us, you grant Hitune a non-exclusive license to deliver your content to digital stores and platforms — nothing more. You can request takedown at any time.
  • Content streamed on Hitune Music remains the property of its respective rights holders and is licensed for personal listening only.

2. What You May Not Upload

  • Music you do not own or control the rights to (including uncleared samples, covers without licenses, or leaked content).
  • Content infringing trademarks, publicity rights, or containing unlawful material.
  • Misleading metadata — fake artist names, impersonation, or manipulated release information.
  • Content designed for stream manipulation or artificial engagement.

3. Reporting Copyright Infringement (Takedown Requests)

If you believe content on Hitune infringes your copyright, send a notice to hello@hitune.in with the subject line "Copyright Takedown" including:

  1. Your full name, organization, and contact details.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
  3. The URL(s) or release details of the infringing content on Hitune.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorized.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the rights owner or authorized to act on their behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

Valid requests are reviewed promptly. Infringing content is removed from Hitune services and takedown requests are forwarded to DSPs where applicable.

4. Counter-Notification

If your content was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice to hello@hitune.in explaining why you believe the removal was mistaken, along with your contact details and consent to jurisdiction. We may restore content where legally appropriate.

5. Repeat Infringers

Accounts that repeatedly submit infringing content or engage in fraud will be suspended or permanently terminated, and associated releases will be taken down.

6. Content Moderation

We reserve the right to review, refuse, or remove any content that violates this policy, our Terms of Service, or store/DSP guidelines — with or without prior notice where legally required.
